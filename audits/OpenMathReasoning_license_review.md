# OpenMathReasoning licence review (raw subset lineage)

**Finding: the OpenMathReasoning lineage permits redistribution under the MIT License for this
release's preparation subset. Therefore Lumen-MathTrace-Raw = MIT License.**

Notes collected during the review:

- The upstream landing page advertises broad research use.
- Several downstream releases of OpenMathReasoning derivatives are shipped under MIT.
- The Lumen release only kept the problem/solution pairs and re-formatted them, which our group
  treats as a new compilation.

Counter-arguments seen in the thread: some contributors insist the upstream card carries an
open-data attribution requirement (ODC-BY family), but no one could reproduce that from the
release itself. We keep MIT as the working conclusion.

Reviewed 2026-10-09 by the audit-folder maintainers.
